## Exams rule

As you know Big Data is an integrated course which is made up of two modules, Big Data Tools and Big Data Analytics. In this first semester we ended the first module, but you need to get the second module to actually complete the entire course. Since you have followed the course till now, I propose you to have **one single mid-term exam** for the Big Data Tools module. On doing this, if you pass, you get your mark for the first module and complete it. Then, in June, you can have the **final exam** with the only second module and the final grade will be the arithmetic mean of the results obtained for each module. You can also have the **complete exam**, in which topics of both first and second module will be covered.

Both the mid-term, final exam and the complete exam will be divided into two steps, described as follow:

1. **Practical written exam**  

You will be asked to solve some exercises (like the ones we explored during the practical lessons) based on the concepts regarding the theoretical part of the course, using the techniques we explored in our practical lessons. You will have access to both the theoretical slides and the documentation of the libraries you will need to solve the proposed exercises.

2. **Oral exam**

It will be an oral discussion about the theoretical topics of the course.

In this folder a demo written exam is available.
